Our flavors, five ways

Five clearly different layouts for the shop-by-category band on the home page. Each one below is a working mock with the real copy and photos: point at it, tap it, tab through it and scroll past it the way a visitor would. All five keep the black band, the LED blue and the white words, and every card opens the live menu in a new tab.

The current pinned rail is described first, for comparison.

NowPinned rail ABento grid BExpanding panels CIndex list DStacked cards EDeli counter

Current: pinned rail

What the site preview shows today: a black band with four dark photo cards, each with a blue LED strip along its bottom edge. On large screens the band pins in place and the four cards slide sideways as the page scrolls, with a “01 / 04” counter and a progress bar; on phones the cards sit in a swipe row, and with reduced motion they show as a plain grid.

Option A — Bento grid

All four categories at once in a fixed grid, with no pinning and no sideways scroll: Flower tall on the left, Concentrates and Edibles side by side, Much more wide underneath; on hover the photo eases in and the LED edge brightens.

On phones: the four cards stack in one column, Flower first and tallest.

Option B — Expanding panels

Four tall panels in a row, like a sideways accordion: the panel you point at or tab to widens smoothly to show its photo and copy, and the others narrow to slim strips that show just the name.

On phones: a vertical stack of name bars; the tapped or focused one opens to show its photo and copy, and a second tap opens the menu.

Option C — Index list + preview

A big numbered list of the four names with their copy; pointing at or tabbing to a row crossfades its photo into the large preview beside the list, and an LED underline slides to that row.

On phones: the list stays, and each row shows its own photo between the name and the copy.

Option D — Stacked cards on scroll

As the visitor scrolls, each category card slides up and stacks over the one before, which shrinks back slightly, so the section answers every scroll; with reduced motion it is a plain list of the four cards.

On phones: the same stack, one card at a time, with the photo above the copy.

Option E — Deli counter

The four categories as glass display cases standing on a lit navy counter, like the deli-style displays in the store: each photo sits inside a glass case with a soft glossy reflection, its name is on a blue deli tag, the LED strip glows under the counter, and the copy sits beneath each case.

On phones: the counter becomes a swipe row, one case at a time with the next one peeking in.
