Questions

Visiting Flavors

When are you open?

Every day, 10AM to 10PM.

Where is Flavors Detroit?

5700 E 8 Mile Rd, Detroit, MI 48234. Get directions (opens in a new tab).

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Order cannabis online for pickup. Open the live menu (opens in a new tab), then pick up in store.

What do I need to bring?

For adults 21+ only. Valid government-issued photo ID required. Medical patients should also bring a valid medical marijuana card.

Do you serve medical patients?

We've got the highest quality flower to suit any medical or recreational need. Questions? Call us at (313) 733-4464.

How do I hear about new drops?

Join our mailing list today! Receive new product alerts before everyone else. You can also follow @flavorsdetroit_ on Instagram (opens in a new tab) for specials, product photos and news.
