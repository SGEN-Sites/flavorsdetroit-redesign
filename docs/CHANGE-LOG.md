## Legend

- **PRESERVE** — kept as-is; content and function unchanged
- **IMPROVE** — same content and meaning, better structure, hierarchy or design
- **REPLACE** — superseded by a better structure (reason required)
- **REMOVE** — deliberately dropped (reason required)
- **ADD** — new section that did not exist on the source

## Imagery

Per-asset decisions are in `audit/image-inventory.json` (681 images: 26 KEEP, 1 REPLACE,
203 OPTIONAL, 451 REMOVE, each with its reason, by `tools/image-decisions.mjs`).

**None generated.** All imagery came from the source site: the logo (resized only), the client's
rainbow icon (favicon and the social card's composition), and the client's own product photos.
The store placeholders are simple drawn shapes of the store's own elements (glass jars on a navy
counter; rainbow awnings, a bright window and a green cross), each labelled "photo coming soon";
they depict no real person or result and are not presented as photographs. The black-and-gold
promotion slides are not used, per the owner's brief.
