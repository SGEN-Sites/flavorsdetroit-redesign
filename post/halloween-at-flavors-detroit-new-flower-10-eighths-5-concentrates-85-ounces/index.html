Halloween at Flavors Detroit: New Flower, $10 Eighths, $5 Concentrates & $85 Ounces

Oct 11, 20263 min readFlavors Detroit

🎃 Detroit after dark

Halloween season has officially hit Flavors Detroit, and this year we’re bringing more than costumes and decorations.

We’ve been loading the menu with new flower, new concentrates, new ounce options and fresh genetics, while extending one of the promotions our customers have responded to the most:

$10 3.5G eighths are staying.

And that’s only the beginning.

This October brings a completely new $85 premium prepacked ounce tier, an expanded $5 concentrate lineup, and another round of fresh flower drops.

New drops. Dark nights. Detroit prices.

👻 The $10 3.5G Promotion Lives On

You asked for it. We’re keeping it going.

Our $10 3.5G flower promotion has been extended, and we’re continuing to add new strains instead of letting the lineup get stale.

New additions include Blue Starburst, Kush Mints, Frog Poison, Warheadz and Nor-Cal Runtz.

That means more choices, more genetics and more reasons to rotate through the menu without paying premium-eighth pricing.

Whether you lean toward sweet and fruity profiles, funky gas, creamy strains or heavier hybrids, there’s something different waiting in the lineup.

$10. 3.5G. New strains. Same Flavors energy.

🕷️ Introducing the New $85 Prepacked Ounce Tier

Something bigger just entered the building.

Flavors Detroit is introducing a new $85 28G prepacked ounce tier for customers looking for a step-up flower option while still keeping the price firmly in value territory.

The first drops include Cherry Meltz, Ghost Mode, Peach Guava and Animal Mints.

Cherry Meltz brings a sweet, creamy profile from Lemon Cherry Gelato and Nor Cal Runtz genetics.

Ghost Mode brings the darker side of the collection with Warheadz × Lemon Cherry Gelato genetics.

Peach Guava leans into juicy tropical character.

Animal Mints rounds out the collection with a minty, sweet and gassy profile.

This tier gives you another choice between bargain ounces and higher-priced premium flower.

28 grams. $85. Premium prepacked flower.

🧪 The $5 Concentrate Lab Just Got Bigger

Our $5 concentrate lineup was already deep.

Now we’re making it deeper.

We’ve added a new collection of budders, cured sugars and sugar waxes with completely different flavor profiles throughout the lineup.

And they join our already-large selection of $5 grams.

More Flavors. More textures. Same $5 starting point.

🌹 Top Shelf Spotlight: Our Most Exclusive Flower

Not everything on the shelf is everyday flower.

At Flavors Detroit, we also carry a small group of high-end, extremely exclusive premium strains for customers who want something more rare, more flavorful, and more elevated from the average menu pick.

These are the strains for shoppers chasing top-tier flavor, standout bag appeal, rich terpene profiles, and a more luxurious smoke. If you’re looking for the best of the best, this is where to start.

These premium drops are in a class of their own:

La Rosa #15

A seductive, flavor-forward balanced hybrid with a floral, candy-sweet character and creamy depth. La Rosa #15 brings a refined profile that feels elegant, smooth, and unforgettable.

Demon Drop

Dark, loud, and dripping with attitude, Demon Drop delivers rich candy sweetness, berry notes, and a gassy finish. This one is bold, indulgent, and made for customers who want something unforgettable.

Crunch Berries

Fun, flavorful, and wildly unique, Crunch Berries brings a sweet berry-forward profile with a smooth, delicious finish. This is a premium flower pick with serious personality.

This October, the lineup is bigger than ever:

Extended $10 3.5G flower. New strains. New $85 prepacked ounces. An expanded $5 concentrate lineup. And more drops still coming.

Whether you’re stopping in for one new strain or stocking up for Halloween weekend, there’s a lot more to discover than what fits into one blog post.

Detroit after dark.

Flavors all month long.
