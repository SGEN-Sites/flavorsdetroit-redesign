Reading the menu

What the letters mean

Strains on the Flavors Detroit menu carry a short code after the name, like Kush Mints (H) or Grape Cake (I).

(I)

Indica

Plants with a shorter, bushier build and broad leaves.

(S)

Sativa

Plants with a taller build and narrow leaves.

(H)

Hybrid

A cross of both. On the menu, H/I leans indica and H/S leans sativa.

Sizes on the menu

1g is a gram. ⅛ is an eighth, 3.5g. ¼ is a quarter, 7g. ½ is a half, 14g. 1oz is an ounce, 28g.

Questions

Good to know

What does (H/I) or (H/S) mean?

Both are hybrids. H/I leans indica and H/S leans sativa.

What is deli-style flower?

Flavors Detroit embodies a sleek, modern style with deli style displays that showcase our premium products with sophistication and clarity. Deli-style flower is sold by the gram, eighth, quarter, half or ounce.

Who can shop at Flavors Detroit?

For adults 21+ only. Valid government-issued photo ID required. Our age check asks whether you are at least 21 years old or a valid medical marijuana patient.

Can I order online?

Yes. Order cannabis online for pickup. Open the live menu (opens in a new tab).

Where can I ask a question?

Call (313) 733-4464, stop in at 5700 E 8 Mile Rd, or message @flavorsdetroit_ on Instagram (opens in a new tab). We can answer your questions.
